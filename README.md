# Workforce Diversity & Inclusion Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The Workforce Diversity & Inclusion Analytics Dashboard is an interactive HR analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of workforce demographics, diversity, inclusion, career progression, training participation, and employee exit trends. It transforms employee data into interactive visual insights that can support workforce analysis and HR decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze workforce gender distribution.
- Understand employee distribution across different age groups.
- Analyze workforce composition by department.
- Examine promotion rates across genders.
- Analyze training participation across job levels.
- Monitor employee exit-rate trends over time.
- Understand workforce diversity and inclusion indicators.
- Provide interactive HR insights through data visualization.

## 📌 Key KPIs

- Total Employees
- Female Employees
- Male Employees
- Diversity Ethnicity
- Training Participation
- Promotion Rate

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Gender Distribution

Visualizes the distribution of employees by gender, including Male, Female, and Other categories.

### Age Group Distribution

Analyzes employee distribution across different age groups:

- <25
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- 55+

### Employees by Department

Shows the number of employees across departments such as:

- IT
- Operations
- Marketing
- Finance
- HR

### Promotion Rate by Gender

Compares promotion rates across different gender groups to understand career progression patterns.

### Training Participation by Job Level

Analyzes completed and non-completed training participation across job levels:

- Entry
- Mid
- Senior
- Leadership

### Exit Rate Trend

Displays the monthly employee exit-rate trend from January to December and helps identify changes in workforce retention patterns.

## 🔎 Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

- Department
- Job Level
- Gender
- Employment Status

These filters allow users to explore workforce diversity and inclusion metrics from different perspectives.
